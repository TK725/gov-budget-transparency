# GUARDRAILS — Budget Transparency

Read before writing anything intended for publication. These exist because the project is politically adjacent and publicly visible.

## Framing
- Non-partisan and constructive, always. No conspiracy-adjacent language ("hidden", "they don't want you to know", "the elites"). The accurate story is the opposite: the data is public, just hard to digest.
- Facts and interpretation are visually and verbally separated. Computed findings ("Titel X deviated 40% from plan") are facts; "this suggests…" is labeled interpretation.
- No claims about individual politicians without a primary source (official document, verbatim quote with link). Prefer institutional framing ("the ministry", "the coalition") over personal framing.

## Data integrity
- Every published number carries its source and retrieval date. No numbers from memory.
- Reproduce headline totals against official figures before publishing any derived analysis (sanity-check gate).
- When official data is revised, published pages get updated, not silently — keep a visible changelog.

## Legal
- DL-DE BY 2.0 attribution on every page that shows BMF data: provider "Bundesministerium der Finanzen", license name, link to license text.
- § 5 UrhG official works: cite the source; don't present modified text of a law as its official wording.
- Website duties before launch: Impressum, DSGVO-compliant setup (prefer no-cookie/no-tracking hosting to keep this trivial).

## Privacy & going public
- Nothing personal about the owner (salary, employer, career details) in any file, commit message, or issue — this repo is destined to become public (Tracker #7a).
- Before flipping the repo public: re-read every file and the full commit history with public eyes; confirm the legal checklist (Tracker #5) is done.
